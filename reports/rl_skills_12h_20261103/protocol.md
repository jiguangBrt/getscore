Updates resume at clean update boundaries, not mid-episode.
- Learning: immutable official pi05_libero base; rank16/alpha16/no-dropout LoRA on 18 action-expert QKVO blocks and 18 gated FFNs, plus critic. Three GPUs compute gradients concurrently; sum globally weighted gradients, clip once, one central AdamW step. Frozen VLM, visual+robot-state critic; ground truth only for rewards/diagnostics.

AdamW(.9,.95),eps1e-8,wd.01; global gradclip1; PPO/valueclip.2, Huber10, criticcoef1; gamma.998/native step, GAE.95/chunk; normalized advantages; no entropy/reference-KL or BC term; post-update approximate-KL safety guard.05.
- Three skill instructions use pick up / place ... upright / pick up ... and place it upright nearby. Flat objects use place ... flat. One shared model, no RPent or LLM in the loop.

Place begins with a real scripted physical grasp, varied wrist, moved near drop zone; setup actions excluded from on-policy data.
